     stream). This proves the race was reduced but not eliminated: the
     wait-goroutine's `cmd.Wait()`/`ptycmd.Wait()` still runs concurrently
     with (not after) the scanner's actual EOF, violating Go's documented
     `StdoutPipe` contract ("incorrect to call Wait before all reads from
     the pipe have completed").
