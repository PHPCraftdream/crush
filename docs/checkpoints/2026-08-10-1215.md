## Decisions

- **`inFlight` per-pump session tracking chosen over lease renewal** as
  BLOCKER-1's primary fix — simpler, fully closes the self-inflicted race at
  the source (the pump can never dispatch two goroutines for one session),
  and needs no new DB query; lease renewal would have needed a heartbeat
  goroutine per execution and still wouldn't have covered the same-tick
  two-distinct-entries path on its own.
- **`ErrCallQueuedNotExecuted` entries are left exactly as leased, neither
  Acked nor Nacked**, relying on the existing 30s lease-expiry mechanism for
  recovery — chosen over immediate retry specifically because
  `mailbox.submit` appends unconditionally on every call with no dedup, so an
  immediate retry loop would append a new duplicate on every pump tick.
- **Did not attempt to distinguish "lease expired from external contention"
  vs. "lease expired from a genuine crash" in `CleanupExpiredLeases`'s
  attempts counting** — accepted as a narrow, honest residual (documented in
  `ErrCallQueuedNotExecuted`'s doc comment) rather than plumbing extra state
  through the lease record to solve a case now much narrower after the
  `inFlight` fix.
- **Regenerated sqlc output via the real `sqlc generate` tool** rather than
  hand-editing `run_queue.sql.go`/`querier.go` directly, to keep the three
  files in sync — consistent with this session's established practice.
